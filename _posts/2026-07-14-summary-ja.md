## [Siri AI is already changing how I use my iPhone](https://www.theverge.com/tech/964714/siri-ai-public-beta-preview-ios-27-hands-on) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Apple has released the first public beta of iOS 27, allowing users to test the new operating system and its integrated Siri AI features powered by Apple Intelligence. Early hands-on reviews suggest the update focuses on refining the user experience rather than introducing revolutionary changes. This release marks a significant step in Apple's strategy to deeply integrate generative AI into its core mobile ecosystem through Apple Intelligence. It affects millions of iPhone users who can now evaluate the stability and utility of these new AI capabilities before the official autumn launch. While iOS 27 supports older devices like the iPhone 11, the advanced Siri AI features require newer hardware compatible with Apple Intelligence. The current beta phase is primarily used to identify quirks and prepare for the final September release.

**Background**: iOS 27 is the upcoming twentieth major release of Apple's iOS operating system, succeeding iOS 26.1.1.5. Apple Intelligence is a suite of personal intelligence features that leverage on-device and server-based models to enhance productivity and creativity across Apple products.
