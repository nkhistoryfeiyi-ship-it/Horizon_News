## [Cloudflare Meerkat - Globally distributed consensus](https://blog.cloudflare.com/meerkat-introduction/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Cloudflare has introduced Meerkat, a globally distributed consensus system built on the asynchronous QuePaxa algorithm, which operates without a central leader. This production implementation aims to provide strong consistency and fault tolerance across diverse geographic regions while mitigating issues caused by network instability. This development is significant because it offers a robust alternative to traditional partially synchronous protocols like Raft, which often struggle with leader elections and latency spikes in global networks. By eliminating the reliance on timeouts and leaders, Meerkat potentially enhances reliability for critical infrastructure services that require strict linearizability. Unlike Paxos or Raft, QuePaxa is an asynchronous algorithm that does not depend on network timeouts, allowing it to make progress even under severe message delays or denial-of-service attacks. It utilizes a randomized core and a hedging mechanism to maintain high performance in normal cases while ensuring safety in adverse conditions.

hackernews · bobnamob · Jul 8, 13:18 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48831565)

**Background**: Consensus algorithms are essential for distributed systems to agree on a single data value, with Raft and Paxos being the most common choices. These traditional protocols are partially synchronous, meaning they assume bounded network delays and use timeouts to detect failures, which can lead to performance degradation or unavailability during network partitions. Asynchronous protocols like QuePaxa remove these timing assumptions, offering stronger guarantees against network unpredictability at the cost of potentially higher latency in worst-case scenarios.
