## [Calif Research Releases WeWorm, a Zero-Click WeChat Worm](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/10/calif-research/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Calif Research has released WeWorm, the first zero-click worm that spreads via WeChat calls on both iOS and Android without requiring any user interaction. The team developed the remote code execution exploit in about two days and built the full worm in one week, using AI assistance for most of the technical work. This demonstration shows that AI can dramatically accelerate exploit development, reducing timelines that previously took larger teams months down to days. It raises significant concerns about AI-augmented threat creation and the future landscape of mobile security. The worm exploits a vulnerability in WeChat calls that triggers remote code execution even if the victim does not answer or interact with the phone. The team provided strategic judgment on target selection and safe testing, while AI handled the bulk of the implementation.

rss · Simon Willison · Sep 10, 00:56

**Background**: A zero-click worm is a self-replicating malware that can infect devices without any action from the user, making it particularly dangerous. WeChat is a widely used messaging platform in China with over a billion users, making it a high-value target for attackers. AI-assisted security research has emerged as a trend, where large language models help researchers identify vulnerabilities and write exploit code faster than traditional methods.
