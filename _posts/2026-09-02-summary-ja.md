## [I trained a small transformer in 1.5hrs and it beats many LLMs](https://mvakde.github.io/blog/44-on-arc-1/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A small autoregressive transformer trained from scratch in just 1.5 hours achieves strong performance on the ARC benchmark, outperforming many larger LLMs. This challenges the prevailing assumption that massive models and enormous training costs are necessary for complex reasoning tasks. It suggests a viable path toward more accessible AI research and deployment, particularly for reasoning-intensive applications. The performance gains came primarily from architectural improvements—SwiGLU activations instead of GELU, RMSnorm instead of LayerNorm, increased data diversity, and scaling from 4 to 8 layers. The author explicitly notes this is not an LLM but a small autoregressive transformer trained from scratch, and clarifies that training on evaluation puzzles is appropriate since ARC is a metalearning benchmark.

Unlike standard NLP benchmarks, ARC evaluates compositional generalization—the ability to transfer knowledge across diverse problem types. Autoregressive transformers are a class of neural networks that predict the next element in a sequence, commonly used in language modeling but applicable to any sequential data.
