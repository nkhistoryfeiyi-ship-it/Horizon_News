## [AirLLM Runs 70B Models on 4GB GPU Without Quantization](https://github.com/lyogavin/airllm) ⭐️ 7.0/10

AirLLM enables running 70B-parameter language models on a single 4GB GPU card without quantization, distillation, or pruning by using layer-wise inference that loads and processes one transformer layer at a time from disk. This achievement lowers the barrier for running large language models on consumer-grade hardware, potentially enabling researchers and developers without access to expensive multi-GPU setups to work with 70B+ parameter models. The layer-wise sharding approach achieves this memory efficiency, but at a significant performance cost — inference runs at approximately 292 seconds per token on an RTX 6000 Ada, making it impractical for real-time applications.

hackernews · Anon84 · Aug 3, 11:15 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49154228)

**Background**: Running large language models typically requires substantial GPU memory; a 70B model in full precision needs over 140GB of VRAM. Common optimization techniques include quantization (reducing numerical precision), pruning (removing model parameters), and distillation (training smaller models to mimic larger ones). AirLLM takes a different approach by streaming layers from disk to GPU on-demand rather than compressing the model itself.
