However, the open-weight release lacks vision support and the 1M context length available in the proprietary Qwen3.8-Max variant, and no QAT-based q4 quantization is provided.

hackernews · Philpax · Aug 12, 15:01 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49273478)

**Background**: Mixture of Experts (MoE) is an LLM architecture that replaces a single dense network with many smaller expert sub-networks, using a router to selectively activate only a subset of experts for each input token. This design allows models to scale to trillions of parameters while keeping per-token compute costs proportional to only the active parameters rather than the total. Quantization is a compression technique that reduces model precision (e.g., from BF16 to FP8 or lower) to save memory and speed up inference, often at a small cost to accuracy.
