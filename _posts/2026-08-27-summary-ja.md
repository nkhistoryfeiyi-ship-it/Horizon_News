This approach has already shown promise in non-small cell lung cancer before expanding to pancreatic cancer.

The ACE3 is Apple's USB-C controller coprocessor, and SPMI is a MIPI standard interface for real-time power management between the SoC and peripheral components. Reverse-engineering these components is necessary because Apple does not release technical specifications for its custom silicon.
