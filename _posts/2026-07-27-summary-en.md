## [EU Proposes Browser-Level Privacy Settings to Eliminate Cookie Banners](https://killthecookiebanner.eu/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The EU Commission has proposed a legislative solution allowing users to set global privacy preferences directly in their browsers, which would automatically signal these choices to websites and eliminate the need for repetitive cookie consent banners. This initiative addresses a significant user experience pain point by streamlining web browsing, while potentially reshaping how digital consent is managed under GDPR by shifting responsibility from individual site interactions to standardized browser signals. The proposal aligns with the Global Privacy Control (GPC) specification, a technical standard that uses HTTP headers and JavaScript APIs to signal a user's preference not to have their personal data sold or shared, aiming for a 'set once, apply everywhere' approach.

hackernews · rapnie · Jul 26, 11:53 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49057175)

**Background**: Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), websites are generally required to obtain explicit consent from users before storing non-essential cookies on their devices, leading to the ubiquitous but often frustrating cookie banners seen across the web. The current system places the burden of managing privacy settings on every individual website, resulting in redundant requests even when a user has already expressed their preferences elsewhere. The GPC mechanism serves as a technical workaround to this fragmentation by providing a universal signal that websites can detect and respect without requiring manual interaction.
